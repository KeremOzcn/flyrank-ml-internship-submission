## Solution Check: 2x - 3 = 9

### ❌ The Solution is Incorrect

The error occurs in **Step 2**. The student subtracted 3 from 9 instead of adding 3 to both sides.

---

### 🔍 Error Breakdown

| Step | Student's Work | Status |
|------|---------------|--------|
| Start | 2x - 3 = 9 | ✅ Correct |
| Step 2 | 2x = **6** | ❌ **Error here** |
| Step 3 | x = 3 | ❌ Follows from wrong step |

---

### ✅ Corrected Solution

**Step 1:** Start with the equation:
$$2x - 3 = 9$$

**Step 2:** Add 3 to **both sides** (not subtract):
$$2x = 9 + 3$$
$$2x = 12$$

**Step 3:** Divide both sides by 2:
$$x = 6$$

---

### ✔️ Verification
$$2(6) - 3 = 12 - 3 = 9 \checkmark$$

---

### 💡 Key Mistake
When isolating the variable, the term **−3** must be *cancelled by adding 3* to both sides. The student appears to have subtracted 3 from 9 (9 − 3 = 6) instead of adding (9 + 3 = 12).

**The correct answer is x = 6.**
